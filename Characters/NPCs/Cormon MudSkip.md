#character #npc #screaming_rock-town #screaming_rock_miners-group 
Rock Gnome Male, he is the foreman of the [[Screaming Rock Miners]]. In charge of running the mines day to day operations, he coordinates the miners and assists with the admin work of running the mines. 
# Personality and Key Features
- While not fully deaf himself like many of the miners, he has had his fair share of exposure to the screaming rocks causing him to be hard of hearing. As a result he often yells when talking with others 
- Cormon carries a distinctive brass horn around his neck which he uses when talking with others in order to hear them better. The horn is engraved with intricate whorls done by Cormon himself.
# Background
- Originally born in the gnomish [[City of Futriming]], Cormon was originally part of a mining company in Futriming who sent him out to the [[Moaning Woods Valley]] to begin evaluating the opportunity for a new mining operation.
- After reaching the [[Moaning Woods Valley]] and sending back reports of the [[Screaming Rock]] there, Cormon's original company decided against the operation. However, Cormon saw the opportunity presented by the strange stones and decided to leave his old company to start his own amongst the other miners already there helping to found the [[Screaming Rock Miners]].
# Relationships
- [[Dysl Exia]]: Cormon's preferred blacksmith in town for all the mine's repairs and tools, he and Cormon have a silent but strong mutual respect for one another.
