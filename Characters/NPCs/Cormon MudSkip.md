Rock Gnome Male, he is the foreman of the [[Screaming Rock Miners]]. In charge of running the mines day to day operations, he coordinates the miners and assists with the admin work of running the mines. 

# Personality and Key Features
- While not deaf himself like many of the miners, he has had his fair share of exposure to the screaming rocks causing him to be hard of hearing. As a result he often yells when talking with others 
- Cormon carries a distinctive brass horn around his neck which he uses when talking with others in order to hear them better. The horn is engraved with intricate whorls done by Cormon himself.
