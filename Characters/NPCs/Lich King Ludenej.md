# Background
- *Ludenej began his path to lichdom studying as a mage under [[The Empire]]. However, early on he became fascinated with the magic behind life itself leading him to begin studying the forbidden magic of necromancy.*
- *Before long, he began paying bandit groups and slavers to kidnap people for his experiments. In which he would torture his victims to bring them to the brink of death in order to try to learn more about the transition from life to death.*
- *Eventually he was discovered by his mage college to be practicing necromancy, but his experiments were not initially discovered. While he was awaiting a tribunal to determine his punishment for studying necromancy, he escaped and fled to the [[Razor Claw Mountains]] before his experiments were discovered.*
- *After escaping from his tribunal, the remains of his experiments were discovered and Ludenej was marked for death should he ever return to [[The Empire]].*
