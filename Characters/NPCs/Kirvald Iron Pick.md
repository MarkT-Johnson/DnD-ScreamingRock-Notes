#character #npc #screaming_rock_miners-group #iron_picks-group 
A female dwarf, she is the leader of [[The Iron Picks]]. Often called "Mother" by the other miners, she was one of the original members of [[The Iron Picks]] that began to mine the [[Screaming Rock]] 200 years ago.
# Personality and Key Features
- Kirvald is the embodiment of cranky old lady, she does not take any crap and cuts right to the point.
	- Despite this, she still embodies her role as surrogate or second mother to the miners keeping a watch out for them all and telling them off when getting out of line.
- While she is deaf, she still speaks, and ensures that everyone around hears what she has to say.
# Background
- When the mines were getting started over 200 years ago, Kirvald was one of the original miners part of the Iron Pick family. She helped to plan many of the original tunnels and knows the mines inside and out.
# Relationships
- [[Cormon MudSkip]]: Kirvald often butts heads with Cormon especially when it comes to getting supplies, often referring to him as "nothing more than a bureaucratic ulcer". Though she is more than happy to let him handle the paper work when it comes down to it.
# Secrets
- The reason Kirvald first came to the [[Moaning Woods Valley]] 200 years ago, she did so to escape the grief of losing of her own child 50 years before to a Necromancer named Dorin Juldeen who was discovered to be experimenting on those he had paid to be kidnapped. However, Dorin escaped before he faced punishment
	- *Dorin unbeknownst to Kirvald fled to the [[Razor Claw Mountains]] where he eventually changed himself into [[Lich King Ludenej]]*
