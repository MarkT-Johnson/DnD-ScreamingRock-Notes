A young Tabaxi wizard from the [[Mage College]]. Bailson has temporarily moved into the [[Town of Screaming Rock]] to study the nearby [[Lorentai]] fortress known as "[[Turtle Shell Fort]]". Though he maintains a room at the [[Silent Goose]], he often spends many days at the [[Turtle Shell Fort]] exploring and documenting everything about the fort as part of his final thesis.
# Personality and Key Features
- Bailson is a young Tabaxi with brown and black fur standing around 4' 5". He is often seen carrying many scroll tubes with his fur covered in ink stains.
