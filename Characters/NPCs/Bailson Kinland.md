A young Tabaxi wizard from the [[Mage College]]. Bailson has temporarily moved into the [[Town of Screaming Rock]] to study the nearby [[Lorentai]] fortress known as "[[Turtle Shell Fort]]". Though he maintains a room at the [[Silent Goose]], he often spends many days at the [[Turtle Shell Fort|fort]] exploring and documenting everything about it as part of his final thesis. Though he has spent many years at [[The Mage College]] studying, he is a wizard of no great power, and has spent more time reading books, scrolls, and tablets than actually casting spells.
# Personality and Key Features
- Bailson is a young Tabaxi with brown and black fur standing around 4' 5". He is often seen carrying many scroll tubes with his fur covered in ink stains. He has a nervous disposition and it becomes painfully obvious upon interacting with him that he has all the social grace of a gazelle amongst a lion pride.
- Bailson is not a person with much conviction and often finds himself stuck on whatever his current project is with little to no sign of him ever approaching some kind of end. Something is always not quite right or needs further exploration before it may be ready for others to review.
# Background
- Bailson is part of a noble Tabaxi family from the northern lands of the [[The Empire|Empire]] known as [[The Fieforence Wilds]].  After it became clear to his family that Bailson had no talent for politics, they sent him off to the [[Mage College]] to get him out of their way.
- Bailson is a representative of the [[Mage College]], though unbeknownst to him the whole reason the [[Mage College]] sent him here was to simply get him out of the class rooms as it became clear after the 6th year of his time at the [[Mage College]], he had no exceptional magical talent whatsoever.
