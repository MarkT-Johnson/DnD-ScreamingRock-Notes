#poi #settlement #screaming_rock-town
The town of Screaming Rock is a mining town nestled in the [[Moaning Woods Valley]] of the [[Razor Claw Mountains]]. While not particularly populous, it is well known amongst the mage community as being the source of the valuable magic stones of the town's namesake "[[Lore/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rock]]".

# Key Features:
##### Deaf Population:
The town grew around the nearby mines which extract the famous [[Lore/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rocks]]. However, due to the screeching of the famous stones of which they mine, many of the residents of Screaming Rock are now deaf. As a result, much of the town's long term residents use a form of hand signals known as "Screaming" to communicate with the miners and each other.
##### Echoing Screams:
Throughout the town, regular piercing screams of seeming agony and fear are routinely ignored by the native residents much to the unnerving of any visitors. These screams come from the nearby mines that extract the town's famous [[Lore/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rock]].
