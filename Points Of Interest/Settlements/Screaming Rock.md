The town of Screaming Rock is a mining town nestled in the valleys of the [[Razor Claw Mountains]]. While not particularly populous, it is well known amongst the mage community as being the source of the valuable magic stones of the town's namesake "[[Lore/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rock]]".

# Key Features:
- __Deaf Population__: The town grew around the nearby mines which extract the famous [[Lore/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rocks]]. However, as a result of working in the mines, many of the residents of Screaming Rock are now deaf. As a result, much of the town's long term residents use a form of hand signals known as "Screaming" to communicate with the miners.
