# Description
An abandoned fort hidden in the [[Razor Claw Mountains]]. This structure is one of the few remnants of the [[Lorentai]] left behind by this ancient race. The fortress gets its name from the turtle shell like domed roof which breaks free from the spines of the [[Razor Claw Mountains]].

# Advanced Knowledge
As one of the few remains of the [[Lorentai]], many treasure hunters, fortune seekers, and grave robbers have picked their way through the structure leaving little behind but broken stones and dust. But one place that remains undiscovered is an entrance to the domed area frequently referred to as the Turtle Shell. Despite many attempts to access this area, no mechanism, doorway, or even digging has revealed a way to access this room. Many have given up hope of accessing it, but every few years a new starry eyed adventure finds their way to the nearby [[Town of Screaming Rock]] to try their hands at exploring this ancient fortress.
