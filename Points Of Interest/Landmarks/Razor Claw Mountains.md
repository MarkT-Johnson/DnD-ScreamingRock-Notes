#poi #landmark
A mountain range known for its sharp peaks and sharper rocks. Its characterized by its man winding valleys creating a natural maze surrounded by towering expanses of rock. Many early explorers of the mountains never made their way back out with the lucky few telling stories of ghostly apparitions appearing within the mountains haunting those that try to conquer the mountains they could not in life.

# Secrets
##### Ghost Stories:
Just about all residents of the Razor Claw mountains have a ghost story to tell of when they encountered an undead apparition whether it was while they were travelling through the winding mountain passes or just out gathering wood or supplies. While outsiders will largely laugh at these tales as merely the wild imaginings of simple mountain folks, the natural magic of the Razor Claw mountains does lend itself to the creation of these specters.
