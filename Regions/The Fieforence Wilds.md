#region #fieforence_wilds-region

The Fieforence Wilds are located in the northern reaches of [[The Empire]]. They are characterized by the chilled winds which blast the plains, cutting to the bone of any creature who wanders them ill prepared for the chill. 

This region was one of the last strongholds against [[The Empire|the Empire's]] expansion due to the brutal conditions forcing [[Imperia]] to change many of their tactics
