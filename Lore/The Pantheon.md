#lore #pantheon-group 
There are 13 gods in the pantheon representing an assortment of alignments and the divine embodiment of various forces of nature. Though these gods are given certain alignments, they follow their representation more than the alignment. I.E Purshung is not always good, as unchecked growth can be bad, likewise Arivast is not always evil as death ends suffering, and clears the way for new tomorrows:

## Fortune, Fate, and Chance: "Hepharon"
___
**Alignment**: Chaotic Neutral
**Embodiment**: All forces of chance, fortune, and the unknown future.
**Followers**: Gamblers, Diviners, Everyday creature.
**Symbol**: Pair of dice with stars for the pips

## Vitality, Growth, and Life: "Purshung"
___
**Alignment**: Neutral Good
**Embodiment**: Growth/life in all forms, both controlled and uncontrolled. New beginnings
**Followers**: Druids, Farmers, Healers
**Symbol**: A tangle of vines choking a tree

## Death, Decay, and Sickness: "Arivast"
___
**Alignment**: Neutral Evil
**Embodiment**: Death/Decay, both in the sense of horror as well as making way for something new. Closure/endings
**Followers**: Necromancers, Druids, Morticians
**Symbol**: A cracked skull with a black rose blooming from the eye socket.

## Invention, Creation, and Tinkering: "Brycanus"
___
**Alignment**: Lawful Neutral
**Embodiment**: The creation/construction of new ideas. Tinkering
**Followers**: Artificers, Trades folk, Alchemists
**Symbol**: An upright hammer passing through a cog

## Exploration, Discovery, and Inspiration: "Ziothernet"
___
**Alignment**: Chaotic Good
**Embodiment**: Seeking of new information, research, discovery, the return of lost things.
**Followers**: Explorers, Librarians, Researchers
**Symbol**: An open book above a ship sailing on the horizon
