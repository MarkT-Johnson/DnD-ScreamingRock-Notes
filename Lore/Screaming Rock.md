#lore #screaming_rock-lore
Mined from the town of [[Points Of Interest/Settlements/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rock]], these magical stones are famous among the mage community as a component for crafting various alarms and notification based charms such as sending stones. 

In their raw form they emit deafening shrieks periodically that can make a man go deaf within only a few exposures, but when manipulated by those experienced with magic can be changed to a variety of other noises more suitable for a wide variety of purposes.
