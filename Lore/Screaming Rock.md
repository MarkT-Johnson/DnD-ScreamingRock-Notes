#lore
Mined from the town of [[Points Of Interest/Settlements/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rock]], these magical stones are famous among the mage community as a component for crafting various alarms and notification based charms such as sending stones. 

When the screaming rock was first found, they were collected in the foot hills of where [[Points Of Interest/Settlements/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rock]] now lays as novelty pebbles that would emit a strange squeak when struck. Eventually, the mage community took an interest and began sending expeditions to collect these pebbles. Further explorations lead to discovering these pebbles were actually old pieces of Screaming rock that had broken down and the larger pieces could be found in the caves of the mountains nearby. This eventually lead to several mining groups setting up semi-permanent camps in the valley. After several years the [[Points Of Interest/Settlements/Screaming Rock|town of Screaming Rock]] took its modern shape and the disjointed groups of miners came together in order to more effectively mine and barter the precious rock leading to the modern [[Screaming Rock Miners]].

In their raw form the stones appear as dark blue glassy rocks that emit deafening shrieks when struck that can make a man go deaf within only a few exposures, but when manipulated by those experienced with magic can be changed to a variety of other noises more suitable for a wide variety of purposes. The extraction of the stones is lucrative but demands a heavy toll as any miner who commits themselves to doing so will quickly go deaf within a few days as they pry the stones free from the mountain's depths.
