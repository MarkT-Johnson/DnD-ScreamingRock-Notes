#group #mage_college-group

The Mage College is the foundation of all magical innovation in [[The Empire]], producing many of the land's greatest and, conversely, worst wizards. Located out of the [[The Mage College]], this group of scholars is the primary scholarly organization through out [[The Empire]] with several different branches of study and outposts.

Originally founded as a college for the study of magic, it has since expanded its repertoire to include many areas of focus with a recent expansion into the gnome [[Magitech]] combining artifice and engineering to achieve some fascinating new arenas of magic to explore.
