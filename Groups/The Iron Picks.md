#group #screaming_rock_miners-group #iron_picks-group 
Originally a family of dwarves that settled in the [[Moaning Woods Valley]] to mine the [[Screaming Rock]], they were one of the first groups to begin mining the stones and often claim to "own" the mines today, though none of the other miner groups would even entertain the idea leading to the group sometimes being called the "The Iron Pricks"

However, given their extensive history with the stones and natural intuition for mining, they are well respected amongst the miners regardless. They are often sought out when it comes to planning new tunnels, navigating treacherous caves, or leading rescues in the event of a collapse.

They are lead by one of their oldest members [[Kirvald Iron Pick]], a female dwarf who was one of the first individuals to mine the stones.
