#group #screaming_rock-town #screaming_rock_miners-group
The group that work the mines in [[Points Of Interest/Settlements/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rock]]. They are lead by their foreman, a male Rock Gnome by the name of [[Cormon MudSkip]].

Working in the Screaming Rock mines, [[Points Of Interest/Settlements/Screaming Rock#Deaf Population|most of their members are deaf]], leading them to instead use hand signals to communicate.
