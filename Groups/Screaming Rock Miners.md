The group that work the mines in [[Points Of Interest/Settlements/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rock]]. They are lead by their foreman, a male Rock Gnome by the name of [[Cormon MudSkip]]. Working in the Screaming Rock mines, [[Points Of Interest/Settlements/Screaming Rock#Deaf Population|most of their members are deaf]], leading them to instead use hand signals to communicate known local as "screaming".

Having formed from several different groups that used to mine the [[Lore/Screaming Rock|Screaming Rock]] separately, many members still feel a part of their original groups:
- [[The Iron Picks]]: Family of dwarves that were among the first to begin mining the stone.
