#group #kobold_tribe-group

Kobolds are a humanoid race of draconic creatures that while sharing many of the characteristics of dragons such as reptilian faces, scaly skin, and sharp claws, are ultimately no where near feared or venerated as much as dragons. Generally treated more like pests, Kobolds are generally considered more of nuisance than any real threat, though their tendency to form larger tribes can make them more of a problem at times.

Generally not the brightest creatures, they tend to act with more confidence and bravery than they have any right too which can lead to them drawing unwanted attention to themselves as they harass other humanoid settlements. Otherwise they tend to be left alone and ignored in the course of day to day life.
