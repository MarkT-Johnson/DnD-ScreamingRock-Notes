#group #lorentai-group

The Lorentai (Lore-en-tay) are an ancient race of people that existed 12 millennium before [[The Empire]] was formed. Little is known about these people, what their culture was like, what race they were, or where they disappeared. What is known is they were humanoid creatures and a few ancient structures from their time remain in the hidden areas of the world.

Most of these structures are abandoned messes with little of value, however some contained advanced magics that ultimately is what lead to the rise of [[The Empire]] in the modern day.
