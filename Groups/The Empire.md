#group #empire-group
The current ruling force of the land. Lead by the [[Imperia]], a god like figure, the Empire has ruled the land for the last 3500 years creating a land of modest prosperity. Early in the Empire's founding it was only one of many fractured states before a discovery of advanced magics that lead to [[Imperia]] rising to power and leading a brutal war taking the land in a many decade campaign swallowing each subsequent state into its growing influence before no state remained.
