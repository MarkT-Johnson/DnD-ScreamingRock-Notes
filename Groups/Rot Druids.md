#group #rot_druids-group

Rot Druids are a group of druids who venerate the process of Life, Death, and Rebirth through the decay and growth of all things. They believe that throughout a creatures life, their soul/aura is allowed to grow and become more perfect while also accruing imperfections/impurities as well, but its through death where that growth is consolidated and any imperfections are able to be removed through rot and decay. From this rot and decay, that progress towards perfection is consolidated so that which is reborn from it is closer to attaining a more perfect being.

Rot Druids as a result venerate death and decay believing it to be the moment of pay off as creatures attain their greater potential. They tend to be followers of [[The Pantheon#Death, Decay, and Sickness "Arivast"|Arivast]], celebrating the chance for new life. While Rot Druids are not inherently evil, their beliefs do tend to put them at odds with many others in [[The Empire]] leading them to be more seclusive and secretive compared to many others. Their alignment with rebirth has often lead them to dabbling in more [[Necromancy|necromantic magics]] often leading to trouble with law enforcement and many people fearing them. Rot Druids as a result tend to prefer isolation preferring to avoid confrontation with others who may not fully understand their beliefs.

Generally the Rot Druids are content for nature to take its course and allow creatures to live their lives, but they do not fear death and as such care little for healing powers and or knowledge. They will rely on chance and fortune to decide their fates or the fates of others when it comes to when they shall die and be reborn. Not all Druids though follow this belief with some called the [[Cult of Ascension]] believing that allowing creatures to live their lives naturally is a waste of time and that through spreading death and decay actively they can accelerate the ascension of other creatures. This difference in belief oftentimes leads to political clashes of the two factions though outsiders will rarely distinguish between the two groups.
